# Forecasting model comparison

Rolling-origin backtest on the demo shops (synthetic data): the last 4 non-overlapping 28-day windows of each shop. Each method only sees sales before its window, and the machine-learning models are retrained for every window. Scores are WAPE (total absolute error as a share of units sold; lower is better), pooled over all windows. Bold marks the best method in each column. Bias is total forecast minus total actual, as a share of actual (negative = under-forecast).

Generated by `backend/scripts/compare_models.py`.
