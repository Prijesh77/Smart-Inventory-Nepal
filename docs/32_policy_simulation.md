# Ordering policy simulation

What each ordering policy would have done over the last 16 weeks of the demo shops (synthetic data). Same starting stock, same customers (each day's actual sales), weekly ordering, orders arrive after each product's lead time, unmet demand is lost. Forecasting models were trained only on days before the window. Stock-out days count product-days on which some customers left without the product. Average stock is valued at cost. Total cost = holding cost of the average stock + a fixed cost per order line + profit lost on missed sales, using the app's cost settings (25% a year to hold stock, the shop setting, Rs 200, per order line); the cheapest policy is in bold.

Generated by `backend/scripts/simulate_policies.py`; policies are described there.

## Demo Kirana Pasal (synthetic data)

2026-07-12 to 2026-10-31, 43 products (A 18, B 14, C 11).
Measured honestly, forecast errors are 1.00x the size the app measures today (median over products).

| Policy | Customers served | Stock-out days | Lost sales (Rs) | Average stock (Rs) | Orders | Total cost (Rs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shopkeeper rule | 96.4% | 235 | 128,740 | 130,422 | 233 | 70,686 |
| App today (95%) | 99.9% | 7 | 6,085 | 328,522 | 152 | 56,247 |
| App, honest errors (95%) | 99.9% | 7 | 6,085 | 330,571 | 152 | 56,404 |
| App + ABC (98/95/90%) | 99.9% | 7 | 1,875 | 339,416 | 153 | 56,906 |
| App, orders of at most 2 weeks | 99.9% | 7 | 4,140 | 237,317 | 232 | 65,053 |
| App, orders of at most 10 days | 100.0% | 4 | 5,070 | 211,909 | 270 | 70,754 |
| App at 90% service | 99.9% | 8 | 2,155 | 317,230 | 152 | **55,034** |

Compared with the shopkeeper rule, the app as it is today had 97% fewer stock-out days and 95% less lost sales, held 152% more stock by value, and cost 20% less in total.
